# The Life of Jesus Christ

A single-page website that chronicles the life of Jesus Christ from the Nativity through the Ascension, drawn from the four Gospels and the Acts of the Apostles.

## Overview

This is a self-contained HTML page — no frameworks, no build step, no external dependencies. Open `index.html` in any modern browser and you're done.

## Structure

| Section | Era | Content |
|---------|-----|---------|
| Nativity | c. 6–4 BC | Birth in Bethlehem, shepherds, wise men |
| Childhood | c. 4 BC – 27 AD | Hidden years in Nazareth, age-12 Temple episode |
| Baptism | c. 27–28 AD | Jordan baptism, forty-day temptation |
| Ministry | c. 28–30 AD | Call of disciples, Sermon on the Mount, teaching |
| Parables | c. 28–30 AD | Good Samaritan, Prodigal Son, Sower, Lost Sheep |
| Miracles | c. 28–30 AD | Water into wine, feeding 5,000, walking on water, resurrections |
| Passion | c. 30–33 AD | Triumphal entry, Last Supper, Gethsemane, trial, crucifixion |
| Resurrection | c. 30–33 AD | Empty tomb, post-resurrection appearances |
| Ascension | c. 30–33 AD | Great Commission, departure |

## Features

- **Zero dependencies** — pure HTML + CSS, no JavaScript, no CDN links
- **Responsive** — adapts to mobile and desktop
- **Sticky navigation** — jump to any section via the top nav bar
- **Scripture quotes** — key verses highlighted throughout
- **Approximate dating** — based on scholarly consensus
